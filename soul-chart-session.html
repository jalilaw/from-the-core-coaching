What It Is

Three lenses.
One sovereign map.

A Soul Chart Session is a 90–120 minute private integrated experience that brings together three distinct lenses: executive coaching, astrology, and tarot. It is not a reading. It is not a therapy session. It is a high-clarity, action-oriented experience designed for leaders who are ready to stop guessing and start moving in alignment with their actual design.

Jayme opens with coaching to establish your clarity request — the question you most need answered. The birth chart then becomes a strategic map, revealing your patterns, your timing, and the design beneath your instincts. Tarot closes the session as intuitive confirmation, distilling the session's insights into a clear directive. You leave with a felt sense of your next right move.
